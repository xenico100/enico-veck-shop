Mongsangnyeo web avatar

Character design, cowl, garment and custom surface work: Mongsangnyeo project.
The web export preserves the existing Blender character including V007 profile
and rim refinements. Source projects and reference images are not included.
The complete custom avatar is not offered as a CC0 asset.
